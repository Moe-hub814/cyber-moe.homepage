Who this is for

Built for career changers, veterans and IT pros who want a real federal cyber role — not another certificate.

The federal space hires for a specific set of skills and processes. Most training skips them. We teach nothing else.

This program is a fit if you…

  • Are transitioning out of the military or a non-technical career and want a clear, structured path into cybersecurity
  • Have an IT, help-desk, sysadmin or network background and want to move into ISSO / security-compliance / cloud-security roles
  • Already have a Security+ or similar cert but can't get past the federal or contractor interview
  • Want to understand RMF, ACAS, eMASS, STIGs and POA&Ms the way they're used on the job — not just defined on a slide
  • Are willing to put in 5–10 focused hours a week for about 6 weeks

It's probably not a fit if you…

  • Want to become a penetration tester, exploit developer or red-teamer — this is a governance, risk, compliance and security-operations program
  • Are looking for a "watch and forget" video library with no accountability or feedback
  • Expect a job to be handed to you — we coach you through the process, but you do the work
  • Need a program that teaches programming from scratch (you won't need it here, and we don't teach it)

What you'll be able to do

Walk into the interview able to do the job.

We teach in job-task language — the same tasks that show up in federal and contractor position descriptions.

Run a system through the RMF

Categorize, select and tailor NIST 800-53 controls, write the SSP, coordinate assessment and get the system to an Authorization to Operate.

Own vulnerability management

Scan with ACAS / Tenable, read STIG and scan results, prioritize findings, and drive remediation with the system owners who actually fix things.

Manage POA&Ms in eMASS

Track weaknesses, milestones and risk acceptance in the tools agencies use, and keep continuous-monitoring artifacts audit-ready.

Secure cloud workloads

Apply federal cloud baselines (FedRAMP, impact levels) to Azure and AWS environments and speak confidently about shared responsibility.

Assess AI systems

Understand how AI and automation change risk, and apply AI-security thinking to assessments — one of the fastest-growing asks in federal postings.

Navigate the hiring process

Understand clearances and sponsorship, contractor vs. federal employment, and how to position yourself for roles you'd otherwise never see.

FAQ

Questions we get every week.

Do I need a security clearance to enroll?+
No. A clearance is not required to enroll or to start your job search. The program explains how clearances and sponsorship work, which roles are realistic without one, and how to position yourself for employers who sponsor.
Do I need to know how to code?+
No. Federal ISSO, compliance and security-analyst roles are about frameworks, tools and process — RMF, ACAS, eMASS, STIGs, POA&Ms and cloud baselines. That's what we teach. You'll use AI tools to move faster, but you won't write software.
How long does it take?+
Most students are interview-ready in about 6 weeks at 5–10 hours a week. The course is self-paced, so you can move faster or fit it around a full-time job or family.
What's the difference between Self-Paced and Mentorship?+
Both include the full curriculum, templates, lifetime updates, a 1-on-1 resume review, mock interviews and job-search support. Mentorship adds 60 days of direct-message access to Moe, a 60-day accountability plan, LinkedIn optimization, ClearanceJobs strategy, clearance-interview prep, salary-negotiation coaching and networking introductions.
Do you guarantee a job?+
No honest program can. What we can show you is a track record: 100+ students placed in federal cybersecurity roles with $20M+ in combined salaries. Mentorship students get a structured 60-day placement playbook and direct coaching until they're through the process.
I'm a veteran. Is this a good fit?+
Many of our most successful students are transitioning service members. You already understand mission, process and accountability; we translate that into the federal cyber skill set and help you leverage any existing clearance.
What about certifications like Security+, CGRC or CISSP?+
Certs help you get past HR filters; this program helps you pass the interview and do the job. Many students pair the program with Security+ (a common DoD 8140 baseline) and we'll advise you on which certs are worth your time for the roles you want.
Can my employer or team enroll?+
Yes. We deliver customized team training for 5–50+ people, on-site or virtual, with certificates and reporting. Use the contact form below to request a quote.
